Who we are

An Algerian integrator serving the power grid

Founded in 2019, Séfar Système is an Algerian company specialising in the integration of electrical and digital systems. From Algiers, we support Sonelgaz, its subsidiaries and industrial companies in building, automating and supervising their electrical installations.

Our core business is digital control systems for electrical substations. Recognised as an Algerian manufacturer by the Mezghena Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Algiers for the manufacture of electrical equipment and solar energy equipment, we are also a certified Schneider Electric EcoXpert partner for Power Automation solutions.

Our organisation is ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certified for building, electrical network and telecommunications works: quality, respect for the environment and the safety of our teams are part of every project. We are classified in category VI of the professional qualification for building and public works, and authorised by Sonelgaz-Distribution to build type D electricity distribution works.
