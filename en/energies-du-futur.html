What we bring

Renewables, connected and controlled

A solar plant or a storage system is only valuable if it is properly connected, protected and supervised. This is our original trade, applied to new energy sources.

  • Solar power plantsStudies, integration of delivery and transformer substations, plant supervision.
  • Grid connectionHV/MV connection substations, decoupling protection, metering and exchanges with the grid operator.
  • Energy storageBattery integration, power conversion and control to smooth production or back up a site.
  • Hybrid sitesCombining solar sources, generator sets and the grid, with automatic priority management.
Made in Algeria

An Algerian manufacturer of energy equipment

Séfar Système is recognised as an Algerian manufacturer by the Mezghena Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Algiers for the manufacture of electrical equipment and equipment related to solar energy and other energy sources.
