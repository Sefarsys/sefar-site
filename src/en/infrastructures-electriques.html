Typical architecture

Two incomers, two transformers, no outage

In a dual-supply substation, each transformer feeds its own half-busbar. The bus coupler and bus riser make it possible to transfer the whole load to a single transformer during maintenance or a fault, without interrupting the feeders.

Our projects comply with the applicable IEC standards, including IEC 62271 for high- and medium-voltage switchgear, IEC 60255 for protection relays and IEC 61850 for communication, as well as Sonelgaz technical specifications.
